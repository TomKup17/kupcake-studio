## 7. When the app connects to the internet

Videos, audio, transcripts and subtitles are processed **only on your computer** and are not sent anywhere.
The app has no telemetry, accounts or analytics. It connects to the internet only in these cases:

- **checking for a new version** – once a day it asks GitHub (`api.github.com`) for the list of releases;
  you can turn this off in Settings → Check for new versions,
- **downloading a model** – only after your consent, from `huggingface.co` (and its CDN) and
  `dl.fbaipublicfiles.com`,
- **links** – when you click a link, the page opens in your browser.

In these cases the server sees your IP address, as with any website. Details are in the README, section
Privacy.
